01 / WHAT I HEAR

The four problems, every time

PAIN 01 "Our site looks smaller than we are." You've delivered $40 million projects and the website looks like a sole trader's. Prospective clients and tender panels both make a judgement in about four seconds, and it is costing you work you never hear about.
PAIN 02 "We can't get our projects online." The site was built in 2019 and adding a completed project means emailing the developer, waiting a fortnight and paying for the privilege. So nobody bothers, and the portfolio is three years stale.
PAIN 03 "Nothing talks to anything." Enquiries land in an inbox. Jobs live in Procore. Invoices live in Xero. Someone re-types the same details three times a week, and the handover documents go out over WeTransfer and expire.
PAIN 04 "We only get work through referrals." Which is fine until a quiet quarter. Search picks up the people who don't already know you — and increasingly they're asking an AI assistant which builder to call, not scrolling Google.
