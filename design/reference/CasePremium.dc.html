PROFESSIONAL SERVICES / COPYWRITING

A site that had to out-write the writer's own clients.

Nikita Morell writes exclusively for architects. As far as I'm concerned, no one's better at what she does — engaging, creative, down-to-earth writing that gets read end to end before it gets put down. The website had to carry that, not dilute it.

01 / THE BRIEF

Leading architecture firms don't build their own websites. Neither should she.

Her clients are architects. They notice spacing. They notice type. They notice when the thing they're reading doesn't hold together. A website that was merely tidy would have quietly undermined the argument she makes for a living.

So the brief was restraint: give the writing room, keep every other element out of its way, and make the whole thing feel considered rather than decorated.

02 / THE WORK
A type system with one jobOne measure, one rhythm, generous leading, and nothing competing with the sentence you're reading. Every page is built from the same handful of text blocks, so it can't drift as she adds to it.
Proof placed where doubt happensClient work and testimonials sit at the exact points a visiting architect starts wondering whether this will be worth the fee, rather than parked in a page nobody clicks.
Publishing she controls entirelyShe writes constantly. Posting an article, adding a client, reordering the homepage — all of it is hers, with no developer in the loop and no way to break the layout doing it.
