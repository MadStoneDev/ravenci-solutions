01 / THE CHALLENGE

A store built for browsing, not for buying

The site they inherited did the basics. It listed products, it took payments, and it looked roughly like every other optics retailer online. What it didn't do was help anyone decide.

Thermal and night-vision buyers are researchers. They compare detection range, sensor resolution, refresh rate and battery life across four brands before they spend $4,000. The old store made them open eight tabs to do it, so a lot of them opened a competitor's instead.

02 / THE APPROACH

Design around the decision, not the catalogue

I rebuilt the theme from scratch on Shopify rather than customising a bought one, so every template earns its place. Brand-led browsing for people who already know what they want, spec-led comparison for people who don't.

  • A homepage that leads with expertise, not the sale
  • Brand-led browsing for buyers who know what they want
  • Product pages built for serious comparison
  • Technical SEO and category content rebuilt at the same time
03 / WHAT I BUILT

Three decisions that moved the numbers

FEATURE 01A homepage that leads with expertiseThe first thing a visitor meets is guidance, not a discount banner. Their knowledge is the product differentiator, so it goes above the fold.
FEATURE 02Brand-led browsing for buyers who knowPulsar, HIKMICRO, PARD and Nocpix each get a real landing page instead of a filter chip, so returning customers reach the shelf in one click.
FEATURE 03Product pages built for comparisonDetection range, sensor, refresh rate and battery life in a consistent table across every brand, so nobody needs eight tabs to make a decision.
04 / THE RESULTS

What changed after launch

Purchases rose 38.5% and conversion 12.5% on comparable traffic, which means the gains came from the buying path rather than more people arriving. Organic sessions grew 57% as the rebuilt category pages started ranking.

The store now does what the team does in person: explains the gear well enough that the customer is confident before they reach the cart.
