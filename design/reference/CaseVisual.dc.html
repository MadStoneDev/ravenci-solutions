01 / THE CHALLENGE

The houses were the argument. The site wasn't making it.

A referral-led builder has a specific problem online. The person arriving already trusts you — a friend sent them — and the site's only job is to not lose that trust. Small photos, cramped layouts and a generic template do exactly that.

They needed the architecture to be the content, at the size it deserves, with enough detail for someone weighing up a seven-figure build.

02 / THE APPROACH

Photography first, structure underneath

Full-bleed project imagery, a typographic system quiet enough to stay out of the way, and a project template the team fills in themselves — brief, site conditions, materials, sustainability measures, photography.

  • Project pages added in minutes, not by emailing a developer
  • Large imagery that still hits the 85+ PageSpeed guarantee
  • An enquiry path designed for referred visitors, not cold traffic
  • Ongoing maintenance so it stays that way
