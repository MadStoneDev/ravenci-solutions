04 / MOTION

Two tiers. Everything respects reduced motion.

TIER A · CSS ONLY · USED EVERYWHERE

Scroll reveals, marquees, hover and focus states, comparison rows. Tailwind utilities plus animation-timeline: view() where supported, falling back to a plain opacity transition. Zero JavaScript, zero PageSpeed cost.

--ease-standard: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)
--ease-exit: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)
--ease-spring: cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.2, 0.64, 1)
--dur-fast: 120ms  (hover, focus)
--dur-base: 220ms  (state change)
--dur-slow: 420ms  (reveal)
--dur-story: 900ms (pinned step)
--stagger: 60ms   (row by row)
TIER B · FRAMER MOTION · EXACTLY THREE PLACES

Dynamically imported, below the fold except the hero, and gated on useReducedMotion(). Nowhere else on the site.

  1. Hero build moment — SVG wireframe assembly, cursor parallax
  2. Pinned “whole stack” story — six steps tied to scroll progress
  3. Platform selector — panel morph between four stacks
Full behaviour, timing and states: the three notes pinned beside the homepage artboards on this canvas.
REDUCED MOTION

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every reveal renders at its final state, the hero paints assembled, the pinned story unpins into a plain stacked list, the gauge shows its number without counting, and marquees stop. Nothing is hidden, nothing needs scrolling to appear.

LOADING FALLBACK · ROUTE RAVENCI · LOADING Hairline progress bar pinned under the nav, raven mark at 40% opacity, plus sr-only "Loading page" for screen readers. Matches the existing route-level loading pattern.
SKELETON · CARD Opacity pulse only, 1.6s, --ease-standard. No shimmer sweep.
FOCUS STATE Focused 2px --ring outline, 3px offset, on every focusable element in both themes. Never removed, never colour-only.
