# Terms of service

Last updated: 9 November 2026

These are the terms you agree to when you buy a service from me. I have tried to keep them plain. If anything here is unclear, email me and I will explain it.

## 1. Who these terms are between

In these terms, **I**, **me**, **my** or **RAVENCI** means Richard Haddad, trading as RAVENCI Solutions. **You** or **your** means the person or business buying my services. **Services** means the work you have asked me to do, as described in your quote, proposal or invoice. **Materials** means anything I need to buy to do the work, such as a theme, plugin, font or stock image licence.

I run RAVENCI Solutions as a sole trader, ABN 35 664 615 205.

## 2. When these terms apply and how you accept them

These terms apply to every service you buy from me, unless we have signed a separate written agreement that says otherwise.

You accept these terms when you do any of these things:

- pay an invoice from me, or
- sign or approve a quote or proposal, or
- confirm in writing (email is fine) that you want me to start.

If you are agreeing on behalf of a business, you are confirming you are allowed to do that.

Please keep a copy of these terms for your records.

## 3. The services I provide

Once you have accepted these terms, I will normally start the work straight away, or on a date we agree. I will do the work:

- with reasonable care and skill,
- to the accepted standards for branding, web design and development, web security, maintenance and SEO, and
- in line with Australian law at the time.

The exact services, and what is and is not included, are set out in your quote, proposal or invoice. I work Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5pm, excluding public holidays.

## 4. Quotes, estimates and prices

I give estimates, not fixed quotations. An estimate is my best assessment of the cost, and it can change if the work changes. Estimates are valid for 30 days unless I say otherwise.

The price can end up higher than an estimate if what you ask for changes, or if the work turns out to be genuinely different from what we agreed and I could not reasonably have known that at the start. If the extra cost will be **10% or less** of the estimate, I will carry on and complete the work without checking with you first. If it will be more than 10%, I will stop and get your approval before doing the extra work.

## 5. Payment

**Project work** is paid in stages. Unless your quote says otherwise, that is a 50% deposit to book the work in, 25% at the first look, and 25% before launch.

**Ongoing services** (hosting, maintenance, Website Care and similar subscriptions) are billed monthly in advance, at the start of each month.

An invoice is due on the date shown on the invoice. Monthly services are due on the date they are issued.

## 6. Late payment

If you cannot pay on time, tell me. I would far rather sort something out than send reminders.

I send reminders to your nominated email address: 3 days before the due date, on the due date, 3 days after, and 7 days after. These are a courtesy. Not receiving one does not change what you owe, so please keep your email address up to date with me.

If an invoice is still unpaid:

- **15 days after the due date**, I add a one-off **$35** administration fee to cover the cost of chasing it, and I let you know.
- **20 days after the due date**, I may pause your services (see below). I will warn you before I do.
- **60 days after the due date**, I may cancel the affected services for good. I will send a final warning before this point.

If I have to recover the debt through a debt collector or legal action, you also cover the reasonable costs of doing that.

### What a pause means

Pausing is not cancelling. When a service is paused:

- your website may be taken offline and your email may stop working,
- I keep your data and your domain safe during the pause, and
- the service starts again once the invoice is paid. In some cases a reconnection fee applies to bring a paused service back online, and I will tell you if one does.

### What cancelling at 60 days means

If an invoice is still unpaid 60 days after the due date, I may cancel the service permanently. After that:

- your data and backups may be deleted, and
- a domain I renew on your behalf may be allowed to lapse, which can mean you lose it.

I will always send a final warning before this happens.

## 7. Your responsibilities

To do the work well I need a few things from you:

- content, information, approvals and access, supplied in reasonable time,
- accurate information, and
- the right to use anything you give me.

You confirm that any text, images, logos or other content you give me is yours to use, or that you have permission to use it. If a claim is made against me because of content you supplied, you cover me for the reasonable cost of dealing with it.

If I am held up because I am waiting on you, the timing of the work may move accordingly.

## 8. Timing

I aim to do the work by the dates we agree or that I tell you. I cannot firmly guarantee a start or finish date, because web projects depend on content, approvals and third parties. Where no date is fixed, I will do the work within a reasonable time.

If I do not start or finish within a reasonable time, you can choose to keep waiting, or you can end the contract. If you end it, you pay for the work done up to that point and for any Materials I am committed to paying for. If you have paid more than that, I refund the difference within 15 days.

## 9. Things outside my control

Sometimes something outside my reasonable control gets in the way, for example a third-party service (a host, registrar, plugin or payment provider) going down or changing, Materials I rely on not being available, content or approvals not arriving from you, or other events I could not avoid.

When that happens I restart the work as soon as I reasonably can. If it causes a long delay, you can choose to keep waiting, or to end the contract on the same terms as in clause 8.

## 10. Cancelling and ending a service

**Ongoing services are monthly, with no lock-in contract. You can cancel any time.** Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for, and I do not refund part months. The one exception is the optional 12-month Website Care contract, which has its own terms, including an early-exit fee, set out when you take it up.

**Project work**: once booked, a project is not something you can cancel part way through on a whim, because I set time aside for it. If we agree to stop a project early, you pay for the work done up to that point and for any Materials I am committed to paying for, and I refund anything you have paid beyond that within 15 days.

I may suspend or stop a service if you do not pay, as set out in clause 6.

## 11. Hosting, domains, email and backups

- **Domains** are registered in your name, so you own them. If I renew or manage a domain for you, that does not change who owns it.
- **Backups**: where hosting or maintenance is part of your service, I take working daily backups. A backup that does not restore is useless to both of us, so they are tested, not just taken.
- **Uptime**: I do not guarantee a specific uptime figure unless your service says so in writing.
- **Third-party providers**: some things run on other companies' systems (hosts, registrars, plugins, email and payment providers). I am not responsible for their outages or changes, though I will help you deal with them.

## 12. Search visibility (SEO, AEO, GEO)

I cannot guarantee a ranking, a position, or an amount of traffic on Google or in AI answers. Search engines and AI systems run their own results and change how they work often. I do the work to recognised standards, and any content I write for you comes with an honest note on where it is weak, but the outcome is not something I or anyone can promise.

## 13. Who owns the work

Once you have paid for the work in full, the designs, code and content I create for you are yours. Until then, they remain mine, and you do not have the right to use unpaid work.

Some parts of a website are not mine to give away. Third-party components (themes, plugins, fonts, stock images and software libraries) stay under their own licences, which I pass on to you. Tools and frameworks I reuse across projects stay mine. Where your site uses RAVENCI Builder, it is covered by its own separate licence, which stands on its own and is not part of your hosting or Website Care.

## 14. Showing your work in my portfolio

I may feature work I have done for you in my portfolio and marketing, on ravenci.solutions and elsewhere. That can include your business name, logo and screenshots of the site. If you would rather I did not, just tell me and I will leave you out. Where I have agreed to keep a project private, I will not show it at all.

## 15. Privacy and your data

I handle your data in line with my privacy policy, at ravenci.solutions/privacy-policy. Where I have agreed that your data is sensitive or needs to stay in Australia, I keep it on Australian servers.

## 16. If something goes wrong

Nothing in these terms takes away any rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot be excluded. Where the law lets me limit what I am responsible for, my responsibility for a failure in the services is limited to doing the work again, or paying the cost of having it done again.

I do not limit my responsibility for death or personal injury caused by my negligence.

Beyond that, I am only responsible for loss or damage that is a reasonably foreseeable result of me breaking these terms. If you are using my services for a business, I am not responsible for business losses, including lost profits or other economic loss.
