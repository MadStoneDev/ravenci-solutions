PHP2Shell: The PHP Security Scare and What You Should Do About It

By Richard · 5 min read · 23 October 2026

PHP is cooked. We're all cooked. What do we do?!

Well, we relax. We massage our earlobes while repeating, "woosah", which helps with neither the PHP issue nor your stress levels. Allegedly.

Don't get me wrong, this is quite a critical security vulnerability and we need to act fast, but it doesn't mean your website is already in trouble. Think of it like keeping your front door closed and locked but the bolt is fragile.

What the LLM happened?

Ok, that jargon slipped out. In plain words:

  • On 20 October 2026, the people behind a programming language that many websites use (PHP) warned the world about two critical security flaws and released an official fix the next day.
  • The first known attack was spotted by Amazon and it took place on 17 October.
